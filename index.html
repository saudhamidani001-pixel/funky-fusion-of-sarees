Our story

Ten years of bringing sarees home

Around ten years ago, I had one simple dream: to bring something different to the saree market.

So many women here love Indian sarees, for the weaves, the handwork and the colours. But the truly special pieces were hard to find locally.

So I went looking for them. With a love for sarees and an eye for designs you don't see everywhere, I began choosing exclusive collections from sellers in India I trust, and bringing them closer to the women who would treasure them.

Ten years on, that is still Funky Fusion Of Sarees: sarees chosen with care, for women who truly appreciate them.

Uzma IbrahimFounder, Funky Fusion Of Sarees

Questions, answered

How does booking work?

Place your order and send 10% in advance by bank transfer (Meezan Bank) or JazzCash. Your saree is imported in about 40 days, and you pay the remaining 90% in cash when it arrives.

Why does delivery take about 40 days?

Each saree is imported for the order it belongs to. We message you on WhatsApp at every step, from booking to your door.

I need it for a date. What should I do?

Tell us the date on WhatsApp before you book. We'll say honestly whether it can reach you in time.

Can I see the saree before I book?

Yes. Message us and we'll show it on a WhatsApp video call, or send more photos and videos.

Is the blouse included?

Yes. Every saree comes with its blouse piece. The saree's page says if it is stitched or unstitched.

What does delivery cost?

Rs. 250 in Karachi and Rs. 300 to every other city in Pakistan.
